## Objectives

From `prd.md`'s success metrics:

- **Adoption** — a user runs `init` once; no additional commands required after that.
- **Context continuity** — a new session reaches working project understanding within minutes,
  not by re-deriving it from scratch.
- **Memory quality** — relevant updates, minimal noise, minimal duplication.
- **Automation** — more than 90% of memory maintenance happens automatically, without a person
  being asked to do it.
